# Sổ Thu Chi 3.2 (December 8, 2026)

## New
- Export your transactions to Excel (.xlsx) from Settings > Data.
- A monthly spending summary is now sent as a push notification on the 1st of each month.

## Fixed
- Fixed a sync issue where some transactions were duplicated on Android 12 devices.

## Important
- The old backup format (.sbk) will no longer be supported after March 1, 2027. If you keep backups in this format, open the app and create a new backup before that date.
